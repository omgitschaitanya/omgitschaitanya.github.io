Instructor Hunt

There are 10 QR codes around the venue, each for one instructor. People scan them in any order. Your Nth unlock always gives the Nth reward, so everyone gets the same reward at the same count. What differs is which instructor you just unlocked and what your collection looks like. Use the buttons to step all three people through their scans.
